# P8 Deferred Obligations and Evidence Boundary

This report is derived status evidence for `DH-P7-P8-EXEC-01`.  It does not
modify H03, H04, or any ADR packet.  A compiled interface is recorded as `I`;
checked theorem terms and finite examples retain their separate `K`/`E`
labels.  No item below claims `R1+` runtime refinement.

## BD-CONTROL

ADR-07 is proposed and acceptance-pending, although its standalone spike is
compiler-validated.  The production package has no authoritative lifecycle
orchestration relation, labelled `Step`, `InFlight` boundary, scheduler
semantics, or complete asynchronous failure calculus.  P7's ranked
two-counter iterator is finite `I`/`K`/`E` evidence for its fixture only.

## BD-STAGING

ADR-08 is proposed and acceptance-pending.  The base/extended staging
correspondence, `AtomicProfile`, stable-image, adequacy, and forward-simulation
contracts are not production modules.  The candidate packet and spike are
inventory evidence (`A`/`I`), not a staging theorem.

## BD-SUPPORT

ADR-09 is proposed and acceptance-pending.  Support as a positive least fixed
point, provider-to-dependent orientation, well-founded support order, and the
support/confluence lemmas remain deferred.  The finite alpha support witness in
P6/P7 does not discharge this blocker.

## BD-SCOPED

ADR-10 is proposed and acceptance-pending.  Typed realm references, resolver
separation, finite overrides, persistent isolate/intercept contexts, metadata
precedence, and flat embedding are not integrated into `STC/`.  The complete
Markdown/JSON/spike packet is compiler-validated but remains candidate `A`/`I`
evidence.

## BD-COEFFECT

The P5 finite/dependent store and observation interfaces are available, but the
full satisfaction/specification and coeffect-mediated operation calculus is not
complete.  Existing `I`/`K` evidence is limited to the declarations and
theorems named by the Definition Ledger; it does not promote pending Section
3.2 rows.

## Runtime refinement

`STC.Adapter` supplies only the one-way R0 abstraction and labelled forward
simulation types.  There is no concrete Cordis state, atom/generation
refinement, loader correspondence, executable runtime relation, converse
simulation, or bisimulation.  Therefore runtime evidence is `R0` seam-only and
`R1+` remains deferred.

## Reopen and superseding-ADR rule

Changing a carrier, failure meaning, alpha action, freshness boundary, or the
one-way simulation direction requires a superseding ADR before production work.
This P8 batch makes no such semantic change.
